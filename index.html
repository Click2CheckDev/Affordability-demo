How it works

One layer between the customer, the adviser and the forms

C2C doesn't just collect reports. It checks them against each other and against the customer, then fills whichever form the firm uses.

  1. Collect. Credit file, Open Banking (several banks), AML, ID check. HMRC is optional: the customer chooses PAYE, SA302 or both, in one consent journey (see the HMRC demo).
  2. Open Banking aggregate. Accounts connected twice are merged, overlapping feeds de-duplicated, transfers between the customer's own accounts paired, and every transaction placed on a budget planner line. RULES · ACCOUNTSCORE MAP · MERCHANT MEMORY · TRAINED MODEL
  3. C2C Data Pack. Every party's credit, banking, AML and ID in one file. Residential and commercial use the same model; a commercial case adds the company, and each director gets the same personal checks.
  4. Validate. What the customer entered is compared with every source: salary and bonus against HMRC payroll, profit against SA302, commitments against the credit file, spending against the bank. Differences are flagged, not changed.
  5. Broker and customer decide. Accept the source figure, keep the entered one with a reason, or categorise what the bank data couldn't place. Every decision goes on the audit trail. NO AI ON A CASE
  6. Fact find and budget planner. Populated and validated, on the lines of the C2C Mortgage Expenditure Form, with where each figure came from.
  7. Fill any template. The C2C form, or the firm's own PDF, Excel or form fields. AI proposes how a new template maps; a person approves it once, and from then on it fills every case. AI PROPOSES · PERSON APPROVES

What's real in this demo: every figure, check and difference was produced by the affordability engine from fictional sample reports. Your clicks replay decisions in the browser, and the Excel files are filled in the browser from the same mapping files the service uses. What isn't: the reports are made up, and nothing is sent anywhere.
